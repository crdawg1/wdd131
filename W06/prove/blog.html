The Magician King

Author: Lev Grossman

The Magician King is the sequel to The Magicians. Quentin Coldwater has achieved many of his dreams and now rules the magical land of Fillory.

My Review

This novel expands on the world introduced in the first book and explores themes of responsibility, maturity, and purpose. The story mixes fantasy, mystery, and character growth in a way that keeps readers engaged.
